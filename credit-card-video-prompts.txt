HOW TO USE
  1. Record the VO first. Re-derive real IN/OUT points into
     video/src/creditcard/beats.ts before generating anything. Timings below are
     ~150wpm estimates, not the cut.
  2. Paste the NEGATIVE PROMPT block below once into the negative field — it
     applies to all 11 clips, unchanged.
  3. Generate A03 FIRST. It is the cleanest full view of the clerk. Regenerate
     until the head is completely blank, save that frame, and feed it as the
     reference image on A03, A05 and A11. (Or reuse the locked AMIR / shopkeeper
     reference frames from the Klarna shoot.)
  4. Clips marked [10s] must go on a model that outputs 10s, then trim to the
     OUT point. Everything else generates at 5s and trims. Never stretch a short
     clip to cover a long scene.
  5. Where a prompt names a camera move, set it with Higgsfield's motion control
     rather than trusting the prompt text. Every move stays slow — this house
     style has no fast camera anywhere in it.
  6. All cards, screens and statements are BLANK on purpose. Every number is
     burned in during the edit, never generated.
