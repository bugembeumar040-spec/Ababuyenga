WHAT THIS FILE IS

The academy-sale film. Why a club sells the kid it raised and keeps the
£70m signing it can't get rid of — book value, amortisation, and the sale
that is one hundred percent profit.

Same architecture as the credit card pack. Two complete, separate ways to
shoot it. Pick one — do not mix them, the idioms don't cut together.

  TRACK A — VIDEO.  11 generated clips, Higgsfield. Highest production
                    value, highest cost, most drift risk.
  TRACK B — STILLS. 11 generated images, moved in the edit. A quarter of
                    the generations, no temporal artefacts, frames reusable
                    as thumbnails, as X cards, and as b-roll later.

House style, character treatment and the faceless rule carry over
unchanged from the finance packs. This one adds a second ban — see RIGHTS.


================================================================================
RIGHTS — the ban that makes this pack postable
================================================================================

Football is not finance. There is a second rights layer on top of the
usual one, and it is enforced harder than the footage is.

NOTHING in this film may contain: a club badge or crest, a competition
logo, a kit sponsor or manufacturer mark, a recognisable real player's
face or likeness, a nameset, an identifiable stadium exterior, or any
frame that could be mistaken for broadcast footage.

Every prompt below asks for unbranded kit, blank boots, empty grounds and
anonymous bodies on purpose. That is not an aesthetic constraint that
happens to be safe — it is the safety, and it happens to be the aesthetic.

The voiceover is where the specifics live. Naming clubs, players and fees
in the VO and in burned-in text is commentary and it is fine. Say it,
never show it.

================================================================================
VOICEOVER — full script
================================================================================

 01  They sold the academy kid. Not because he wasn't good enough.
 02  Every player a club buys goes onto the books at a price.
 03  And that price gets spread across the length of his contract. A
     slice each year.
 04  Four years in, most of it's been written off. What's left is his
     book value.
 05  Sell him, and only the gap above that book value counts as profit.
 06  Now take the one they raised. Ten years in the academy, and no
     transfer fee ever paid for him.
 07  So there's nothing on the books to write off. His book value is
     zero. And zero is the entire point.
 08  Forty million for the academy kid is forty million of profit. Forty
     million for the signing might be five.
 09  Same fee. Same afternoon. Same player, if you only watch the
     football. Different line on the sheet.
 10  He was never a pathway.
 11  He was the cleanest asset they had. And on the one weekend the club
     needed a number, he was the only one that worked. That's why he
     left.

================================  TRACK A  =====================================
                        VIDEO — 11 CLIPS (HIGGSFIELD)
================================================================================

SETTINGS: 9:16 · 1080p · 5s or 10s per the scene map · text-to-video.

CONSISTENCY: this film has no character to hold, which removes the drift
risk that dominates the finance packs. What must hold instead is the
GROUND — the same academy pitch, same weather, same hour. Generate CLIP 06
first, it's the cleanest wide of the pitch. Save that frame and feed it as
the reference image on clips 01, 06, 10 and 11.

PALETTE: overcast morning, wet grass, low sun behind cloud. Desaturated
greens, cold greys, one warm floodlight source. No stadium spectacle —
this is a training ground at 7am, not a matchday.

HIGGSFIELD NOTE: where a prompt names a camera move, set it with the
platform's motion control rather than relying on prompt text — the presets
hold far better than described motion. Keep every move slow. This house
style has no fast camera anywhere in it.

NEGATIVE PROMPT — paste once, applies to all 11 clips:

facial features, eyes, mouth, nose, lips, eyebrows, face, portrait, club
badge, crest, emblem, sponsor logo, kit manufacturer logo, competition
logo, nameset, shirt number, recognisable footballer, celebrity likeness,
identifiable stadium, broadcast overlay, scoreboard graphics, text,
lettering, numbers, logos, brand names, watermark, signature, cartoon
outlines, cel shading, flat vector, anime, 2D illustration, distorted
hands, extra fingers, extra limbs, morphing objects, warping architecture,

CLIP 01 · 0:00.0–0:05.0 · 5.00s · HOOK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A single empty locker in a youth changing room, door hanging open, one
bare wooden peg inside, a plain unbranded training shirt left folded on
the bench below it. Cold morning light through a high frosted window. Dust
in the air. Everything else in the room is in shadow.

MOTION: very slow push in on the open locker. Nothing else moves.

NOTE: the emptiness is the hook. Do not add a person, a bag, or a second
open locker — one vacancy reads as a decision, several read as a clearout.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CLIP 02 · 0:05.0–0:10.2 · 5.20s · THE BOOK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A heavy ledger open on a desk in a dim office, thick ruled columns, all of
them completely blank. A fountain pen resting in the gutter of the spine.
Low warm desk lamp from the left, the rest of the room unlit. Shallow
depth of field, the far page falling out of focus.

MOTION: slow drift left to right along the ruled columns.

NOTE: blank columns. The £42,000,000 goes on in the edit and it must land
on empty paper.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CLIP 03 · 0:10.2–0:15.0 · 4.80s · THE SPREAD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Five identical squares of morning light thrown across a dark wooden floor
by a window frame, evenly spaced, each one slightly weaker than the last
as they recede into the room. Dust turning slowly in the beams.

MOTION: locked off. Only the dust moves.

NOTE: this is amortisation as an image — one thing cut into equal
diminishing parts. Five squares, one per contract year. Count them.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CLIP 04 · 0:15.0–0:19.0 · 4.00s · BOOK VALUE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The last and faintest of those squares of light alone on the floorboards,
almost gone, the rest of the frame in darkness. A single boot lying at the
edge of it, unbranded, worn, laces loose.

MOTION: extremely slow push in, closing on the boot.

NOTE: continuity with 03 — same floor, same grain, same light direction.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CLIP 05 · 0:19.0–0:24.0 · 5.00s · THE GAP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Two stacks of plain unmarked paper on a dark desk, one tall and one short,
side by side, lit hard from one side so the height difference throws a
long shadow. Clean negative space above both stacks.

MOTION: slow rise, camera lifting to hold both stacks and the empty space
above them.

NOTE: leave the top third of the frame empty. The subtraction sum lives
there.

CLIP 06 · 0:24.0–0:29.2 · 5.20s · THE KID

Wide of an empty academy training pitch at first light. Grass heavy with

================================================================================

One pack is a week of the account, not one post:

  SAT/SUN  The 55s film. Native upload, 9:16, captions burned in — never
           a link, X suppresses them and the audio-off audience is most
           of it.

           04 and 07 attached to the beats that carry numbers.
  ANY DAY  When an academy sale actually happens — and in a window one
           will — quote it with scene 10's line. That is the post that
           gets the account found.

HOOK VARIANTS for the thread and the card, if the first doesn't move:

  · Your club didn't sell him because he wasn't good enough.
  · The academy isn't a pathway. It's a balance sheet.
  · A £40m academy player and a £40m signing are not the same £40m.
  · The cheapest player at the club is the most valuable one to sell.
