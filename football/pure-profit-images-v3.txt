AUDIT OF THE ORIGINAL SCRIPT — three findings, all fixed below

 1. THE SCRIPT WAS OVERWRITTEN BY ~40%.  This is the structural one. The
    old VO ran ~180 words across 55.0s. At the deliberate pace this house
    style uses (~145 wpm) that scene map holds ~130 words. Scene 11 was
    29 words in 7.9s — 220 wpm, near auctioneer. Every scene was being
    rushed to fit, which is exactly what kills a slow-camera film.
    FIXED: rewritten to 128 words. Nothing else about the map changed.

 2. THE NUMBERS CONTRADICTED THEMSELVES.  Scene 05 burned in PROFIT
    £31.6M; scene 08 then said the same signing returned "five". Both
    can't be true, and the collision beat — the most screenshotted frame
    in the film — was the one carrying the wrong figure.
    FIXED: rebased on £50m / 5 years / sold after 2. Round, coherent,
    and the collision lands at a clean 4x. See NUMBERS.

 3. SCENE 11 BURIED ITS OWN HOOK.  "The one weekend the club needed a
    number" is the true mechanism but it was vague. Academy sales cluster
    before the accounting deadline, and saying so is what makes the film
    argue with people.
    FIXED: "in the week the accounts were due."

Rights ban, palette and negative prompt carry over unchanged from
pure-profit-prompt-pack.txt. Say it, never show it.

RENDER IT AS ONE TAKE, NOT ELEVEN. V3 gets unstable on short fragments —
it needs roughly 250+ characters of context to hold delivery, so eleven
one-line generations will drift in tone and cost far more credits than
one continuous read. Generate the block below whole, then cut to the
scene map in the edit. Silence between beats is free and it is the house
style anyway.

SETTINGS
  Model      eleven_v3
  Stability  Natural  — Creative drifts on a read this flat; Robust
                        ignores the tags entirely
  Voice      low, unhurried, no radio warmth. This film is a man reading
             an accounts sheet, not a documentary narrator.

TAG WARNING. Only well-supported tags are used below. V3 occasionally
*speaks* a bracketed tag it doesn't recognise — if you hear one, delete
it rather than swapping in a synonym. Pacing here is carried by the
ellipses and em-dashes, not by tags; that is deliberate and more reliable.

THE READ — paste this whole block
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[serious] They sold the academy kid. Not because he wasn't good enough.

Every player a club buys goes on the books at a price. That price is
spread across his contract... a slice a year.

Two years in, twenty million's gone. What's left is book value.

Sell him — and only the gap above that counts as profit.

[curious] Now the one they raised. Ten years. No fee ever paid.

[whispers] Nothing on the books. Book value... zero. And zero is the point.

Forty million for him is forty million profit. For the signing — ten.

Same fee. Same player, if you only watch the football. Different line.

[serious] He was never a pathway.

[whispers] He was the cleanest number they had, in the week the accounts
were due... That's why he left.

IMAGE PACK — 11 FRAMES
================================================================================

Stills only. Each frame is moved in the edit; the MOVE line is the whole
of the animation. A still pushed slowly for five seconds is
indistinguishable from a generated clip and has no temporal artefacts.

The set rhymes with the VO on one rule: ACCOUNTING OBJECTS FOR THE
MECHANISM, THE GROUND FOR THE BOY. Ledger, light, paper and stacks carry
the argument; the pitch, the locker and the tunnel carry the person. They
alternate, and they meet at frame 08.

PALETTE: overcast morning, wet grass, low sun behind cloud. Desaturated
greens, cold greys, one warm lamp. Training ground at 7am, never a
matchday. Photographic throughout, shallow depth of field, no
illustration.

GENERATE FRAME 06 FIRST — it anchors the ground. Reuse its seed on 01,
10 and 11 so the weather and hour hold across the film.

A single empty locker in a youth changing room, door hanging open, one
bare wooden peg inside, a plain unbranded training shirt folded on the
bench below. Cold light through a high frosted window, dust in the air,
the rest of the room in shadow.
MOVE: slow push in.
NOTE: one vacancy reads as a decision. Two read as a clearout.

MOVE: slow drift left to right along the columns.
NOTE: blank columns — the fee lands on empty paper.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Five identical squares of morning light thrown across a dark wooden floor

MOVE: locked off.

MOVE: slow rise.
