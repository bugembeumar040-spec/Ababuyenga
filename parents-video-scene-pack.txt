WHAT THIS FILE IS

The visual build. Every shot in the video, timecoded to the script, with the
image prompt for each.

This is a STILLS format, not a video format. The source channel does not
generate motion — it generates illustrations and moves the camera across them in
the edit. That is why their 30-minute videos are economically possible and why
they hold up: an illustration does not drift, warp, or grow a sixth finger
halfway through a shot. Do not put this on a video model. You will pay ten times
as much for a worse film.

  101 generated stills → 121 shot events. The gap is re-framing: a wide held for
  16 seconds, then a push into a detail of the same image, is two shots off one
  generation. Re-frames are marked [RF] and name their parent image.

01 — HOUSE STYLE (calibrated off the source channel's own frames)
================================================================================

The look, from their thumbnails and frames:

  MEDIUM      Ink line and watercolour wash. Visible pen contour, loose washes
              that break the line, dry-brush texture, paper tooth showing
              through. Hand-made, not rendered.
  GROUND      Warm cream parchment, #F4EFE2. The illustration sits in a band
              with torn deckled edges against it.
  PALETTE     Deep indigo-navy #22375C — all linework and shadow masses
              Warm ochre #D08A28 — every light source, lamp, sun, fire
              Muted terracotta #B5623F — accents, textiles
              Sage/olive #7C8560 — vegetation, some garments
              Bone #E8DFC8 — cloth, walls in light
              No other colours. This restraint IS the channel's signature.
  LIGHT       One warm source, always. Lamp, doorway, window, low sun. Deep
              navy everywhere it does not reach. Never flat daylight.
  FIGURES     Loose and gestural, never portrait-detailed.
  EDGE        Torn paper edge on the panel, ragged, never a clean rectangle.

PASTE THIS AS THE STYLE SUFFIX ON EVERY PROMPT BELOW:

  +STYLE = "Ink and watercolour illustration, visible pen linework in deep
  indigo-navy, loose translucent washes bleeding past the contour lines,
  dry-brush texture, warm cream parchment paper showing through. Restricted
  palette: deep indigo-navy, warm ochre, muted terracotta, olive-sage, bone
  white. One warm light source, deep navy shadow everywhere else. Hand-painted
  storybook plate, torn deckled paper edge. 16:9. No text, no lettering, no

02 — THE FACE RULE — READ THIS BEFORE YOU GENERATE ANYTHING
================================================================================

NO FACE APPEARS ANYWHERE IN THIS VIDEO. Not one, not in the background, not
small, not blurred.

This is not a limitation imposed on the format. It is already IN the format —
look at the source video's own thumbnail: the seated family around the table are
rendered as blank navy shapes with no features at all, and it reads as
deliberate style, not as absence. We take their background convention and make
it the whole convention.

How to write it, every time a person appears:

  · From behind, or from the side beyond three-quarters
  · Cropped at the shoulder, or at the chest
  · Hands, forearms, feet, the back of a covered head
  · At enough distance that features were never going to resolve
  · Where a figure must face camera: features simply not rendered — the head a
    smooth unmarked shape, exactly as their own thumbnail does it

HARD PROHIBITIONS — no exceptions, no workarounds, no "silhouette version",
no "light where the face would be", no "from behind is fine for him":

  ✗ THE PROPHET ﷺ. He does not appear in this video in any form whatsoever —
    not a figure, not a shape, not a light, not a hand, not an empty space
    labelled as him. In chapter 11, where the script places him at his mother's
    grave, THE FRAME CONTAINS NO PERSON AT ALL. It is a road and a horizon.
    That shot is S111 and it is written that way on purpose. Do not "improve" it.
  ✗ ANY PROPHET. Same rule.
  ✗ NAMED COMPANIONS. Sa'd, Asma, Mu'awiya ibn Jahima, Abu Bakr — none of them
    are depicted as persons. Their scenes are carried by objects, hands, and
    rooms. Mu'awiya ibn Jahima is S62 and S67; Sa'd is S86 and S90; Asma is
    S94 and S95. Not one of those five frames contains a person.
  ✗ THE KA'BA WITH A CROWD, or any frame that could read as a devotional image.
  ✗ ANGELS, JINN, ANY REPRESENTATION OF THE DIVINE, light-from-the-sky
    theophany imagery. The source channel uses that device constantly. We never
    do. It is the single biggest visual difference between their video and ours
    and it is not negotiable.

JURAYJ (chapter 07) is a man from a past nation in a narration — not a prophet,
not a companion. He is still faceless, still from behind, still at distance,
and the chapter is deliberately carried by his mother's approach and the ruin
of the building rather than by him.

TRACK B — THE FIGURE-FREE CUT. Some of your audience holds that no complete
human figure should be depicted at all. That is a real position and you should
decide where the channel stands BEFORE you spend money on generations. Every
shot below marked [B-OK] already contains no complete human figure and needs no
change. Every shot marked [B-ALT] has a figure-free alternative written into it
on the second line. Running Track B costs you 18 alternate prompts and nothing
else — the film survives it, because this topic is unusually rich in objects:
the empty chair, the cooling tea, the doorway, the walking stick, the folded
blanket, the water jug, the unworn spectacles.

  Track A = 101 stills as written.  Track B = 83 as written + 18 alternates.

03 — ARABIC WORD CARDS (typeset in the edit — NEVER generated)
================================================================================

Image models cannot render Arabic. Every one of these is set as a title card in
your editor over the cream ground. A model asked for Arabic will produce
convincing-looking nonsense and that error, on a channel called Clarity in the
Quran, is the one you never come back from.

Nine cards. Format is identical each time: Arabic large and centred in a proper
naskh face, transliteration beneath in small caps letterspaced, meaning beneath
that in navy sentence case. Hold 4 seconds. No animation beyond a 3% push.

  CARD  TIME     ARABIC              TRANSLIT       MEANING LINE
  ----  -------  ------------------  -------------  --------------------------
  C1    1:38     وَقَضَىٰ رَبُّكَ         WA-QADA RABBUKA  "And your Lord has decreed"
  C2    5:05     وَبِالْوَالِدَيْنِ إِحْسَانًا  WA BIL-WALIDAYNI  "and to parents, good
                                     IHSANA         treatment"
  C3    6:35     وَهْنًا عَلَىٰ وَهْنٍ       WAHNAN 'ALA WAHN  "weakness upon weakness"
  C4    7:05     وَلِوَالِدَيْكَ          WA LI-WALIDAYKA  "...and to your parents"
  C5    7:52     أُفٍّ                UFF            a breath of irritation
  C6    10:30    قَوْلًا كَرِيمًا         QAWLAN KARIMA  "a noble word"
  C7    11:30    بِرّ  /  عُقُوق        BIRR / 'UQUQ   expansive goodness / the cut
  C8    20:35    وَصَاحِبْهُمَا مَعْرُوفًا    WA SAHIBHUMA   "and keep company with them
                                     MA'RUFA        with kindness"
  C9    26:20    رَبِّ ارْحَمْهُمَا      RABBI-RHAMHUMA  "My Lord, have mercy on them"
                 كَمَا رَبَّيَانِي صَغِيرًا   KAMA RABBAYANI   "as they raised me when I
                                     SAGHIRA        was small"

  C5 is the centrepiece of the film. Give it the full 4:3-ratio hold, ochre on
  cream, and let the narration land on silence after it.
  C7 is the only split card — birr left, 'uquq right, a hairline rule between.
  C9 is the closing card and it also becomes your pinned comment.

  PROOFING. Every one of these must be checked by an Arabic-literate person
  against a mushaf before publish — vowel marks included. I have set them from
  the standard text of 17:23-24, 31:14-15 and 2:83, but a diacritic dropped in
  a font substitution is invisible to me and glaring to your audience. This is
  the single highest-risk item in the whole production.
