## The CTA chain

Each video's close is a promise the next one has to land. Track it or the chain
breaks.

- Klarna closes: **"Follow — the fix is next."**
  → The credit-card pack is what pays that off. Its back third (scenes 09–11) has to
  be the fix, not more diagnosis. If cutting for time, cut from the middle, never
  from the payoff.
- Credit card closes: **"Follow — the halal card fix is next."**
  → **The next video owes a concrete halal card alternative.** That is an outstanding
  promise to the audience.
