WHAT THIS FILE IS

The shipped build of the salaam Short. Unlike the credit-card pack, the VO was
already recorded when this was made, so NOTHING here is estimated — every IN/OUT
below was derived from word-level STT alignment of the delivered ElevenLabs
track (86.67s of speech, 88.20s of picture).

The written script says [00:00–01:00]. The delivered read is 86.67s. The
performance pauses are much longer than the script's estimate, so the script's
own timestamps were discarded and the cut was built against the real audio.

Track A (video clips) was not shot. Stills were the right idiom here: the tone
is reverent and unhurried, generated motion adds drift risk on architecture and
hands, and every frame doubles as a thumbnail or as b-roll later.

MODEL: nano_banana_pro, 9:16, 2k, 2 credits/frame. 25 generations total
(22 keepers + 3 reshoots). Everything else is ffmpeg.


================================================================================
CONTENT RULES — NON-NEGOTIABLE, THEY SHAPED EVERY PROMPT
================================================================================

  1. NO depiction of Allah. No anthropomorphic divine imagery of any kind.
  2. NO depiction of the Prophet ﷺ — not a figure, not a silhouette, not a
     back-turned stand-in. Scene 09 carries him by PLACE (the green dome at
     Madinah), never by person.
  3. NO AI-generated Arabic. Every image prompt ends with an explicit ban on
     text, letters, numbers, calligraphy and signage. Image models garble Arabic
     script, and garbled Qur'anic text is not a cosmetic defect.
     All three Arabic texts on screen are set in real type (see TYPE below).
  4. FACELESS by default. Hands, backs, silhouettes, over-shoulder, crops.
     Where a face crept in, the frame was reshot (see RESHOOTS).
  5. NO music bed. The audience splits on music; the pauses in this read are
     dramatic and carry themselves. A brown-noise air bed sits at ~-35 dB purely
     so the long gaps do not read as a dead file.

================================================================================
HOUSE STYLE — appended verbatim to all 22 prompts
================================================================================

  Shot on 35mm film, cinematic documentary still, vertical 9:16 composition.
  Warm golden natural light, dust in the air, soft volumetric light shafts.
  Muted earthy palette of sand, terracotta, olive green, deep teal shadows and
  warm gold highlights. Shallow depth of field, gentle halation on highlights,
  fine organic film grain, slight vignette. Reverent, quiet, unhurried mood.
  Absolutely no text, no writing, no letters, no numbers, no calligraphy, no
  signage anywhere in the frame. Nobody looks at the camera.

The palette is bound further in the grade so the 22 frames read as one shoot:
contrast 1.05, saturation 1.03, shadows lifted warm and the blue channel pulled
down slightly, vignette PI/4.6, temporal grain.


================================================================================
RESHOOTS — what failed first time, and why
================================================================================

  04  Rows of worshippers came back as a rotated top-down plane of tiny
      featureless white figures — uncanny, and it read as a glitch. Refixed by
      naming the camera ("eye-level ground-view"), the ground plane, normal
      human proportions, and atmospheric perspective.

  09  Madinah read as a game render, and carried a white polaroid border.
      Refixed with "real documentary photograph", "true optical depth",
      "authentic stone and tile texture", "not a render and not an
      illustration", plus an explicit "full bleed, no border, no frame".

  15  The man's face was visible and the offered hand was anatomically loose.
      Refixed with "only the back of his head visible and never his face" and
      "anatomically correct hand with exactly five natural fingers".

Borders showed up on several frames. The Ken Burns push starts at ≥1.00 zoom on
a 2x-upscaled source, so any residual edge is cropped out in the move.


================================================================================
TYPE — captions and the Arabic
================================================================================

Latin      Montserrat 800.
Arabic     Amiri Quran (aliftype/amiri 1.001), shaped by PIL's raqm/HarfBuzz
           layout engine with direction='rtl'. Harakat survive intact.
ﷺ          Montserrat has no U+FDFA, so the renderer detects any glyph outside
           Montserrat's cmap and falls back to Amiri mid-line at 1.25x size.
           Without this it renders as a tofu box — it did, on the first pass.

  kind       size  colour    y      used for
  ---------- ----- --------- ------ ---------------------------------------
  hook        118  white     1320   the four hard beats
  en           84  white     1335   narration
  scripture    80  cream     1335   anything inside quotation marks
  ref          46  dim gold  1370   AL-AHZAB · 44, SAHIH MUSLIM, AN-NISA · 86
  ar          190  gold       880   the ayat, centre frame
  ar_small    170  gold      1300   السلام عليكم over the embrace

Every cue auto-shrinks to fit 980px and sits on a 4x-stacked 24px-blurred black
plate, so it holds over the bright colonnade frames as well as the dark ones.
All captions sit between y=880 and y=1400 — clear of the TikTok/Reels chrome.

THE ARABIC IS THE USER'S OWN SCRIPT TEXT, PASTED, NOT RE-DERIVED:

Loudness is on the social target, so no platform will re-gain it.

REBUILD:  python3 render_caps.py && python3 build.py
  beats.py       scene map + 38 caption cues (the timing source of truth)
  render_caps.py cues -> 38 transparent 1080x1920 PNGs
  build.py       Ken Burns -> dissolves -> captions -> grade -> VO mix

build.py runs in four stages and writes an intermediate at each one, so a
caption change only costs the caption pass, not the Ken Burns pass.

ONE TRAP WORTH KEEPING: a caption starting at t=0 must not be given a negative
-itsoffset. ffmpeg silently drops the input and the cue never appears. The hook
went missing on the first render for exactly that reason; the pad is now
clamped to min(0.35, start).
