CUT FROM v2 — DO NOT GENERATE
================================================================================

  A02 · "NEVER YOURS" · the document tug-of-war, Adam and the Institution both
        holding one blank contract.

  WHY. Its entire content is the keeper/owner argument, and the rewritten script
  removes that on accuracy grounds: on a UK PCP the V5C IS issued in the
  customer's name — they are the registered keeper, the finance company is the
  legal owner, and the V5C states in terms that it is not proof of ownership.
  "The logbook was never in your name" is falsifiable in thirty seconds by
  anyone who has held one for a financed car.

  DO NOT TRY TO RE-CAPTION IT. Two figures holding one document reads as
  disputed ownership and nothing else, and ownership is no longer the claim.

  A02 was also the designated thumbnail and the ADAM reference frame. A07 now
  does both jobs — see the A07 block.
