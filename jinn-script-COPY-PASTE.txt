Janeen. An unborn child. Hidden in the womb where nobody can look.

and told it was a character.

AUDIT — v1 AND v2 WERE WRONG IN TWO DIFFERENT WAYS
================================================================================

v1 was a lecture. Long sentences, no contractions, citations landing before the
claim. That diagnosis was right and those fixes carry into v3.

v2 fixed the prose and broke something else, which is why it still read badly.

  I COPIED A SHORT'S RHYTHM INTO A TEN-MINUTE VIDEO.
  v2 ran 130+ sentences at a 4.6-word mean — actually shorter than your finance
  VO. That machine-gun cadence is right for 55 seconds and punishing across ten
  minutes. There's no rise and fall in it, so it flattens into a different kind
  of robot voice. v3 breathes: short lines where a point lands, long ones where
  the listener needs to be carried. The numbers are further down.

  THERE WAS NO STAKE.
  This is the bigger one. Your finance Short grips because it has a villain, a
  victim and money on the table — the industry has a secret word for you, and
  you're losing. v2 was four corrections in a row. Being corrected is
  satisfying. It isn't gripping, and nothing in it cost the viewer anything.
  This topic has a real stake sitting right there and v2 walked past it: people
  pay healers to remove jinn. v3 opens on that, and the Sulayman story stops
  being an interesting fact and becomes the moment the fraud collapses.

  THERE WAS NO PERSON IN IT.
  Not one "I" anywhere. v3 has exactly one, placed where it does the most work —
  admitting I believed the fallen-angel version my whole life. That single
  admission converts the video from correcting the viewer to standing beside
  them. One is the right number; more would be self-indulgent.

WHAT ELSE CHANGED

  THE OPEN NO LONGER STARTS WITH A STORY THEY ALREADY KNOW. v2 opened on the
  angels bowing to Adam — a scene the entire audience can recite. v3 opens on
  an accusation with money in it, promises four verses, and makes the Sulayman
  story a debt the video owes them. The Adam scene now arrives at 2:30, where
  it's a setup for the reveal instead of a flat recap.

  THE SUBSCRIBE ASK IS EARNED NOW. v2 said "if that landed, subscribe." v3 puts
  it right after the fraud line, adds a share prompt aimed at a specific person
  the viewer is thinking about, and promises something that "changes what you do
  tonight." Same position, far more reason to act.

  THE FOUR CORRECTIONS NOW COST SOMETHING. Each one lands on the viewer rather
  than on the subject: Iblis had a will, and so do you. Jinn have believers and
  disbelievers, like your street. They can't see your future, so nobody can sell
  it to you. Seeking refuge doesn't protect you, it owns you.

  SCENE-SETTING ADDED AT TWO POINTS. The night the jinn heard the recitation,
  and the traveller in the valley. Both were abstract in v2. People remember
  places, not propositions.

  THE ENDING NOW PAYS OFF ITS OWN SETUP. "Hold onto that, it comes back" in the
  shaytan beat now genuinely returns in the An-Nas beat — with the whisperer who
  has a face, a name and a phone number, tying back to the healer in the open.
  v2 planted that and never collected.
