WHY v1 SOUNDED OFF — the voice diff, measured
================================================================================

Your Shorts VO and v1 of this script were run through the same counter. The gap
was not a matter of taste, it was structural:

                        YOUR SHORTS      v1 (off)      v2 (this file)
  mean words/sentence       5.3             6.4            4.6
  median                      5               5              4
  longest sentence           14              35             17
  sentences under 8 words    84%             69%            83%
  contractions                3               0             18
  "you" / "your"              6              25             26

v2 now sits on top of your Shorts profile on every line that matters.

Two of those did the damage in v1.

  ZERO CONTRACTIONS. v1 said "is not," "does not," "they are," "it is" —
  everywhere, without exception. That single choice is most of what made it
  sound synthetic. Nobody speaks that way. Your finance VO says "isn't,"
  "didn't," "you were never." v2 uses contractions throughout.

  THIRTY-FIVE-WORD SENTENCES. v1's worst offenders were textbook constructions:
  "And when he needed a throne carried across a great distance, one of them, an
  ifrit, a powerful one among the jinn, said..." Your Shorts never go past 14.
  Every long sentence in v2 is broken at its natural breath.

Four more rewrites, all in service of the same thing:

  CITATION NOW COMES AFTER THE PUNCH, NOT BEFORE. v1 opened beats with
  "Surah Ar-Rahman, verse fifteen. He created man from clay..." — the reference
  arrives while the listener is still waiting for a reason to care. v2 leads
  with the claim and drops the reference behind it, the way your finance VO
  states the thing and only then names it: "That's the actual term."

  THE SPOKEN NUMBERING IS GONE. v1 announced "Number one." "Number two." like
  form headings. v2 lands "That's one." at the END of each correction instead —
  it counts exactly the same, feeds the pinned comment exactly the same, but it
  lands as a punch rather than a heading. The on-screen 1-OF-4 counter in the
  scene pack carries the visual numbering, so nothing is lost.

  SIGNPOSTING CUT. "So.", "Now.", "Which brings us to...", "Sit with that." —
  all gone. Your Shorts never announce a turn, they just turn.

  THE LISTENER IS IMPLICATED THROUGHOUT. v1 was mostly third-person description
  of jinn. v2 pushes "you" into the load-bearing lines: "you were told," "They
  were here before you," "Iblis had what you have," "Like you," "They're
  standing next to you." That is the move your finance script runs on — "you
  were never the customer," "You didn't ask. It arrived."

AUDIT — carried forward from v1, still true
================================================================================

The two substantive corrections found in the v1 audit are preserved in v2:

  DIVING IS SAD 38:37, NOT SABA. Saba 34:12-13 doesn't mention diving. v2 keeps
  the corrected line: "Sad describes them in four words. Every builder and
  diver." Verified against the text.

  "THREE WORDS," NOT FIVE. كَانَ مِنَ الْجِنِّ is three words. v1 originally said
  five before this was caught.

Also preserved: the cold open no longer makes the listener do arithmetic, and
the subscribe ask still sits immediately after the Sulayman reveal rather than
at the end.

ONE NEW CHECK IN v2. The single longest sentence is now the "anyone selling you
the unseen" line. It is deliberately the longest thing in the script and it sits
two lines after the peak — the length is doing work there, letting the listener
come down before "That's three." If you trim anything, don't trim that.

WHAT DID NOT CHANGE, AND WHY. Every verse reference, every transliteration and
every theological claim is identical to the audited v1. This was a rewrite of
rhythm, not of content — so the eighteen verified citations and the accuracy
notes in the scene pack all still stand without re-checking.
