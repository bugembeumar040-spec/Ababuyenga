PART 2 — THE FORMULA, EXTRACTED
===============================================================================

Derived from 8 full transcripts. Five beats, in this order, every time:

  BEAT 1 — COLD OPEN ON THE MUNDANE (0–8s)
    Two permitted forms only:
      (A) ANECDOTE: "Ada..." / "There was a man in his 30s..." — always with
          a useless-sounding specific detail (his age, the year, the coin
          denomination). The uselessness is what makes it feel true.
      (B) DIRECT QUESTION: "Siapa di antara kita tahu...?" / "Who among us
          knows...?" — a question the viewer CANNOT answer. Never rhetorical
          in the lazy sense; it must genuinely stump them.
    Never opens with "Islam says", a statistic, or a definition.

  BEAT 2 — FORENSIC ESCALATION (8–35s)
    The engine of the whole format. Take the mundane thing and narrow into
    it with increasing, almost obsessive specificity. The leaf video does
    six escalating steps. Each step must be MORE specific than the last, not
    merely another example. Specificity, not volume.

  BEAT 3 — THE PIVOT (35–50s)
    One sentence turns the camera onto the viewer. Signature constructions:
      "Lalu apakah Anda berpikir...?"  -> "So do you really think...?"
      "Tidakkah Anda tergerak...?"     -> "Aren't you moved to...?"
      "Nah, itu bahaya."               -> "Now THAT is the danger."
      "Hati-hati dengan..."            -> "Be careful with..."
    This is where the like-rate is won. 9–10.5% likes on this channel is
    conviction, not entertainment.

  BEAT 4 — THE NAMED AUTHORITY (50–70s)
    Quran cited by surah AND verse number, or a hadith, or a named scholar
    (Ibnul Qayyim appears by name). Arabic first, then translation. Exactly
    one to two anchors — the channel never stacks a pile of evidences.

  BEAT 5 — THE LANDING (70–end)
    Short. Absolute. Quotable. Often a triplet or a flat negation:
      "There is no alternative. Not wealth, not facilities, not popularity,
       not power. Only dhikr."
      "Allah is All-Seeing, All-Hearing, All-Knowing."
      "Never underestimate Allah's power to help us."
    Never a call to action. Never "like and subscribe". The landing IS the CTA.

TONAL RULES OBSERVED
  - Direct address to the room ("hadirin", "jemaah") — creates in-the-room
    intimacy. Your English equivalent: "listen", "look", or nothing at all.
  - Self-deprecating humour as a pressure valve mid-video ("Gubrak. If anyone
    was floored, it was me.") — keeps a heavy topic watchable.
  - Frequently ends on a soft laugh or exhale. Do not end clean and hard.
  - Runtime 44–90s. Shortest performed best per-view.

               bank marks. Always "unbranded matte black card, blank surface".
               This is both a Higgsfield quality issue and a trademark issue.

PART 5 — ELEVENLABS PRODUCTION NOTES

MODEL       Eleven v3 (audio tags in square brackets only work on v3; v2 will
            read them aloud as literal text — this is the single most common
            mistake, check your model selector before you generate).

CRITICAL — DO NOT GENERATE SCENE BY SCENE
v3 is unstable on very short inputs. Several VO lines above are under 100
characters and will come out with random emotion drift if generated alone.
Generate in the four BLOCKS below (each is comfortably over 250 characters),
then cut the audio to picture in your editor. This is the difference between a
professional read and an AI read.

  BLOCK A (scenes 1–5):
  [curious] Quick question. [short pause] What did you buy on your credit card
  four months ago? [thoughtful] You don't remember. [pause] I don't remember
  either. [serious] But the bank remembers. The item. The store. The time — [short
  pause] to the second. The terminal you tapped. Which hand you held the phone in
  when you tapped it. [pause] Four months. Four years. [pause] Every coffee. Every
  "one time only". It is all still sitting there.

  BLOCK B (scenes 6–9):
  [thoughtful] So think carefully. [pause] If a bank — [slowly] run by men who
  will die — [pause] keeps a record that perfect... [serious] do you really think
  Allah keeps a lesser one? [pause] And here is the difference. The bank's record,
  you settle with money. [pause] [slowly] Allah's record is opened on a Day when
  nobody has any. [serious] Look at your statement. That small number in the
  corner. [pause] The minimum payment. [sharply] That number is not mercy. It is
  engineering. Pay only the minimum, and something you could not even remember
  buying four months ago... [pause] you are still paying for four years from now.

  BLOCK C (scenes 10–12):
  [serious] Allah said: [slowly] do not consume riba, doubled and multiplied.
  [pause] Surah Ali 'Imran, verse one hundred and thirty. [thoughtful] Doubled.
  [short pause] And multiplied. [serious] That is not poetry. [slowly] That is a
  compound interest table. [pause] The Prophet, peace be upon him, cursed the one
  who consumes riba... [short pause] the one who PAYS it... the one who records
  it... and the two who witness it. [pause] [slowly] And he said: they are all the
  same.

  BLOCK D (scenes 13–14):
  [sad] Every sin of the martyr is forgiven. [long pause] [whispers] Except debt.
  [thoughtful] So before the next tap, [short pause] ask one question. [whispers]
  Is this worth being asked about? [pause] [exhales] Cut it. [pause] Today.

VOICE SETTINGS
  Stability      Natural (~50). Do NOT use Robust — it flattens the tags.
                 Creative drifts and will invent emotion you didn't ask for.
  Similarity     ~75
  Speed          0.92–0.95. The gravitas in this format comes from pace.
  Style exagg.   Low. The tags are doing the work already.

VOICE CASTING
  Male, 30s–40s, warm low-mid chest register, unhurried, slight rasp. You want
  "someone who has been through this", not "narrator". Avoid the bright
  American podcast voices — they kill the reverence.

TAG DISCIPLINE
  - "ﷺ" will NOT be pronounced. In BLOCK C it is written out as "peace be upon
    him" deliberately. Keep it that way in the audio, keep the ﷺ glyph in the
    on-screen text.
  - Tags shape delivery probabilistically, not deterministically. Expect to
    re-roll 2–4 times per block. Keep the seed of the take you like.
  - CAPS on "PAYS" in BLOCK C is intentional — v3 reads capitalisation as
    emphasis. Don't lowercase it.
  - Ellipses "..." create natural hesitation. They are load-bearing here.
  - Don't stack more than two tags in a row; v3 starts ignoring them.

  [serious] Last week we talked about Klarna. [pause] A lot of you commented
  that you don't use it. [short pause] [curious] Fine. [pause] Open your
  banking app. Look at the second card.
  Visual: phone screen glow lighting a face from below in a dark room, no
  readable UI, slow push in.

HOOK D — "the weight" (most scroll-stopping, least on-formula):
  [curious] A credit card weighs five grams. [pause] Five grams of plastic.
  [serious] It has no engine. No voice. It cannot follow you anywhere. [pause]
  [slowly] So explain how it got into your marriage.
  Visual: macro of a card on a jeweller's brass scale, hard raking light.

My call: run HOOK C first. It converts your existing Klarna audience and the
"look at the second card" line is a physical instruction, which spikes
retention because people actually do it.
