# JINN — YouTube packaging

Audited by `video/tools/audit-packaging.py`. Every quoted phrase is checked
against `video/media/vo-words.json` — the transcript of the actual voiceover —
and every chapter timestamp against the cut in `beats.ts`, so nothing here
promises something the video does not deliver or points at a moment that is not
there.

---

## What the niche is doing

Reverse-engineered from what currently ranks and performs on this topic. Two
distinct clusters, and this video sits in the gap between them.

**Cluster A — the explainer.** Format-led, length-in-title, safety qualifier in
brackets.

- *Every Jinn Mentioned in the Quran Explained in 17 Minutes*
- *4 Types of Jinn Explained in 13 Minutes (What Islam Actually Says)*
- *Jinn in Islam Explained in 4 Minutes*
- *HIDDEN TRUTH ABOUT JINN IN THE QURAN AND SUNNAH* — Yasir Qadhi / One Islam

**Cluster B — the exposé.** Money, fraud, "inside".

- *Fake Islamic Healers Scam Millions — How to Spot Fake Ruqya*
- *Inside The Secret World Of Islamic Exorcism*

**The gap.** Cluster A explains and never accuses. Cluster B accuses and rarely
argues from the text. This video does both: it builds four verses and then
turns them on the man with the phone number. That fusion is the packaging
angle, and it is also just what the video is.

**Two traps in this niche:**

1. **"Jinn" alone collides with the 2019 Netflix drama** — a Jordanian series
   with its own search demand and press. Same class of problem as the Klarna
   pack's note about "riba" and the Royal Institute of British Architects. The
   title must carry a disambiguator (`Quran`, `Islam`, or a Surah name).
2. **Cluster A's "Explained in N Minutes" is saturated.** Competing there means
   competing with Yasir Qadhi's reach on his own format. The argument-led title
   is the cheaper win.

## TITLE

### Primary

```
The Jinn Couldn't See a Dead King. So How Did He See Yours?
```

59 characters — survives mobile truncation. It is the video's own thesis, it
poses a question that cannot be resolved without watching, and it is honest:
that is literally the argument the film makes at 06:03.

> **Trade-off, stated:** no `Quran` or `Islam` keyword, so it wins on
> click-through and not on search. Use it if the channel gets meaningful
> browse/suggested traffic. If this upload has to earn its views from search,
> take Alt 1.

## THUMBNAIL

### The constraint nobody can design around

**This film has no faces in it.** Not a stylistic preference — the shot pack
forbids them in every one of its 64 negative prompts, and Sulayman, the jinn
and the Prophet are never depicted. The standard Islamic-explainer thumbnail is
a shocked face plus three words, and that route is closed.

So the thumbnail has to win on one object, one idea, and typography. That is
survivable — the film already contains the single strongest object available:
the staff with a hole eaten through it.

The film's own peak, and the only image that poses the video's question without
explaining it.

### Rules for all three

- **Four words maximum.** At the 210×118px it is actually served at on mobile,
  a fifth word is unreadable.
- **Keep the bottom-right corner clear** — the duration stamp sits there.
- **Never generate the text.** Every one of these prompts negative-prompts
  lettering on purpose; the type goes on in the edit at full resolution, where
  it stays sharp and can be changed without regenerating the art.
- **Never generate Arabic.** Same rule as the film: it comes from a verified
  mushaf or it does not appear.
- Export 1280×720, under 2MB, sRGB.
