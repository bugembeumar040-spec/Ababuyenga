CLARITY IN THE QURAN — "THE ONE SOUND THE QURAN FORBIDS YOU TO MAKE"
READY-TO-PASTE PROMPT PACK — style suffix expanded, negative prompt on every shot
Derived from parentsvideoscenepack.txt section 04. Nothing here is new creative —
it is the same shot list with +STYLE resolved so each block is one clean paste.
==============================================================================

HOW TO USE

  · Each block is a complete prompt. Copy the PROMPT paragraph into the prompt
    field, the NEGATIVE line into the negative field. Nothing to append.
  · If your tool has no negative field (Midjourney, some Firefly flows), the
    negative is repeated once at the foot of this file in --no syntax.
  · [RF] blocks are re-frames. NO GENERATION. They are camera moves made in the
    edit on a still you already have. They are listed so the timeline stays whole.
  · TRACK B ALTERNATE blocks are the figure-free versions. Use them INSTEAD of
    the shot above, not as well. Decide Track A or Track B before you spend.
  · Arabic cards C1–C9 are typeset in your editor. Never generated. Never given
    to an image model. They appear below only as timeline markers.

COUNT: 101 stills to generate (+1 thumbnail). 20 re-frames need no generation.
18 Track B alternates are swaps, not additions.
