HOW TO USE

Each block below is copy-paste ready: a PROMPT and a NEGATIVE, plus the aspect
ratio. Nothing else needs looking up.

  · 9:16 shots (S01–S22) are the two Shorts. 16:9 shots (L01–L13) are the
    long-form.
  · 5 of the 35 contain characters. They are flagged with a full-width banner
    and listed in the index below. Shoot those in the order given — two of them
    are reference frames that lock everything else.
  · 4 more contain hands but no faces. Also flagged, because hand anatomy is a
    reject criterion.

IF YOUR FLOW BUILD HAS NO NEGATIVE PROMPT FIELD. Some Google image models
dropped the separate negative channel. If you can't find the field, don't worry
about it — every prompt below has already been strengthened so it holds up
without one. See the note on what changed, immediately below.

WHAT I CHANGED FROM THE PACKS — two targeted edits, nothing else

  1. FACELESS HEADS. Every character prompt keeps the packs' original
     "COMPLETELY BLANK featureless head" line in caps, and now also carries a
     real-world referent: an artist's wooden mannequin / a milliner's head
     block. Naming an object that genuinely has no face works far better than
     instructing a model not to render one. Both phrasings are present because
     belt-and-braces costs nothing here.
  2. BLANK SURFACES. On the shots where a printed surface is the subject —
     cards, statements, screens, tags, doors — the prompt now describes
     unprinted stock as a material state ("bare cream stock, as it exists
     before the printing stage") rather than only asking for the absence of
     text. Same reasoning.

Palette, lighting, lens, aperture, depth of field, framing and materials are
untouched from the packs. That's the house style and it needed no conversion.

NUMBERING. S01–S11 are the Deadbeat stills, S12–S22 the halal card stills,
L01–L13 the long-form. 35 in total, one image per ID.

REJECT CHECKLIST — run on every frame before you accept it
================================================================================

HARD FAILURES — reject immediately
  [ ] Any text, digits, lettering, logo or signage anywhere, including in
      soft-focus background. Number one reject.
  [ ] Any facial feature on any character — even faint, even implied. A shadow
      that reads as an eye socket is a reject.
  [ ] Extra fingers, fused fingers, more than two hands.
  [ ] Palette drift — navy not #1E3A5F, terracotta not #E85F42, cream gone
      yellow or pink.

STYLE FAILURES — these will not cut against the Klarna footage
  [ ] Fully photoreal with no matte-clay quality, OR fully cartoon with no
      material realism. The house style is the hybrid.
  [ ] Flat or diffuse lighting. Every frame has ONE hard key and a real
      directional shadow. No visible shadow means reject.
  [ ] Background not falling to near-black.
  [ ] Deep focus — everything should have a shallow-focus plane.

FRAMING
  [ ] 9:16: subject in the lower two-thirds, clean caption headroom above.
  [ ] 16:9: subject on a third, empty quadrant preserved. Centred = reject.

CONSISTENCY
  [ ] Characters identical across every frame — same clothing, proportions,
      head form. Drift between shots is what kills this format.

ONE LAST RULE. Shoot S03 and S10 first and get them perfect before anything
else. Every other character frame depends on them, and a mediocre reference
propagates into all five.
