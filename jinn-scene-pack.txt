WHAT CHANGED FROM THE PREVIOUS PACK — read this, it has downstream consequences

The style is inverted. The pack was near-photorealistic cinematic stills on a
near-black ground. It is now 2D hand-drawn ink-and-watercolour illustration on
textured aged parchment — the explainer look, not the photography look.

Four things that follow from that, and they are not optional:

  THE GROUND FLIPPED. Parchment is now 65–75% of every frame; near-black is
  gone as a base. Everything that was "deep near-black falloff" is now open
  warm paper. This is the single biggest change and it drives the next three.

  ON-SCREEN TEXT FLIPPED WITH IT. Text was parchment on black. It is now sepia
  ink on parchment. The 1-OF-4 counter at 40% parchment would be invisible on
  the new ground — it is now ink at 55%. Full spec at the bottom of the file.

  THE THUMBNAIL FLIPPED WITH IT. Parchment-white lettering on a dark plate is
  dead; S20 is now a light plate and the type goes to heavy sepia ink. Full
  spec at the bottom.

  CAMERA LANGUAGE IS GONE. No focal lengths, no f-stops, no shallow focus, no
  bokeh. A watercolour has no lens. Composition is now stated as crop and
  layout — close crop, flat overhead, wide establishing, split frame.

WHAT DID NOT CHANGE. The 13 beats, the timings, the 30 shot slots, every MOVE,
the optional cuts, the verse cards, and all four theological rules. Those rules
are not style preferences and a style change does not touch them. Sulayman is
still never depicted. Jinn are still never depicted. Arabic is still never
generated. No horror grammar.

THE RULES — these are not style preferences
================================================================================

THE FOUR THAT CARRIED OVER

  NO FIGURES ANYWHERE. Not one person, hand, or silhouette in 30 shots. The
  reference look permits faceless back-turned figures; this film does not use
  them in the main pack. If you want them, there is a controlled variant block
  further down — read its conditions before you touch it.

  SULAYMAN IS NEVER DEPICTED. Not as a person, shadow, or shape on a throne.
  Beats 08 and 09 are carried entirely by objects and empty architecture. The
  staff in S20 stands alone with nothing holding it.

  JINN ARE NEVER DEPICTED. No creatures, no eyes, no smoke-with-a-shape. They
  are conveyed as absence and as heat bending the air. Draw one and you've made
  a monster video and inherited the monster audience, which does not subscribe
  to tafsir channels.

  ARABIC IS NEVER GENERATED. Every image model garbles it, and garbled Quranic
  text in a Quran video ends your credibility in one comment thread. All Arabic
  is burned in during the edit from a verified mushaf. Every prompt says no text.

  NO HORROR GRAMMAR. No blood red, no green, no crawling shadow, no candle
  corridor. Ember orange is a warm pigment, not a horror red — keep the
  distinction. It's the whole differentiator against what already ranks for
  this keyword.

THE FIVE THAT COME WITH THE NEW STYLE

  PARCHMENT IS ALWAYS VISIBLE. Textured aged paper with real tooth, showing
  through every wash somewhere in frame. If a shot reads as a solid painted
  rectangle, it's wrong. The paper is the film's ground and its throughline.

  LIGHT IS UNPAINTED PAPER. The brightest value in any frame is bare parchment
  with nothing laid on it. Never paint white, never add a glow. A shaft of
  light is a clean-edged wedge of raw paper; a lit surface is where the wash
  stops. This is what makes S02, S03, S04, S27 and S30 hang together.

  NIGHT IS A WASH, NOT BLACK PAINT. S03, S14, S17, S21 and S25 are night
  scenes. Lay a thin deep indigo-slate wash and let the paper tooth read
  straight through it. A dead black field breaks the series in one shot.

  ONE INK WEIGHT, HELD. Loose confident contour line, slightly uneven, with
  visible pen pressure. Not a mechanical outline, not a heavy comic outline.
  Lock it on S02 and judge every later shot against that frame.

  WASHES SHOW THEIR EDGES. Visible wet edges, granulation settling in the
  hollows, dry-brush breaks where the brush skipped. That evidence of hand is
  the entire reason this style reads as made rather than generated. Any shot
  that comes back smooth and even, regenerate it.

TWO MOTIFS, AND THEY COLLIDE

  THE LATTICE (mashrabiya). The carved screen that hides what's behind it while
  passing light. That is the root ج-ن-ن made drawable, and it is the perfect
  subject for this style — ruled pen geometry over bare glowing paper.
  Beats 02, 06, 12, 13.

  THE TABLE. New in v3, because the script now opens on a transaction — someone
  paid a healer. Cash, a phone, two chairs, nobody there. Beats 01, 10, 12.

  They meet in beat 12, on "some of it is sitting across the table from you."
  The lattice light falls across the table for the only time in the film. That
  shot is the visual thesis. Don't let it be ordinary.

PALETTE
  parchment ground  #E8DCC8   the paper — 65–75% of every frame, always visible
  raw paper light   #F4EBD9   the lightest value, unpainted. Never white paint.
  sepia ink         #3A2E24   every contour line, and all on-screen text
  shadow wash       #6E7A80   translucent grey-blue, the only shadow colour
  night wash        #2B3440   deep indigo-slate, laid thin, paper reading through
  ember             #E8873C   what they're made of — beats 03 and 09 ONLY
  muted gold        #C9A227   brass, lamplight, lattice edge
  garden green      #123B36   beat 02 ONLY, on the janna cognate

  Ember in two beats and green in one is the discipline. Spray either everywhere
  and both stop meaning anything. Shadow is grey-blue every single time — the
  moment shadows start going warm brown in one shot and cool in the next, the
  series stops looking like one hand made it.

LIGHT   One implied sun, low and raking, from the same side all film. Shadows
        are single flat translucent washes with a clean edge, thrown long. No
        rendered falloff, no ambient occlusion, no second light source.
MOTION  Nothing fast. Pushes are 5–10% total travel across a whole beat, eased
        out. No shake, no whip pans, no snap zooms.
        OPTIONAL, and use it sparingly: this style supports a line-reveal — the
        ink contour drawing itself on before the wash appears. It is the idiom
        of the reference video. Four shots maximum across the film, or it stops
        being an accent and becomes the gimmick. S02, S12, S20 and S28 are the
        four that earn it.
