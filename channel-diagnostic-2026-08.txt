================================================================================
CAUSE 1 — LONG-FORM HAS NO SURFACE TO APPEAR ON
================================================================================

Suggested-video traffic across the entire channel, 90 days: SIX VIEWS.

Long-form does not live in the Shorts feed. It lives in Browse (Home) and
Suggested. To place a video there, YouTube's recommender needs two inputs:

  (a) a history of THIS channel's long-form holding watch time, and
  (b) an audience with long-form watch history on THIS channel.

You have neither. So each long-form upload gets a seed test of a few hundred
impressions, produces no click-and-hold signal, and stops. And because it
produced no data, the NEXT long-form upload starts from the same zero. Four
uploads, four cold starts, no accumulation.

This is not a penalty, a strike, or shadowbanning. It is a cold start that
restarts every time. The only thing that ends it is long-form that holds
watch time — which cannot happen until enough people see one, which is the
loop that has to be broken deliberately. It will not break itself.


================================================================================
CAUSE 2 — THE SUBSCRIBER BASE IS A SHORTS AUDIENCE AND IT DOES NOT CONVERT
================================================================================

195 subscribers produced 11 views from the subscriber feed and 5 from
notifications, across 21 uploads in 90 days.

Shorts subscribers are swipe-feed subscribers. They do not visit Home. They do
not have the bell on. They subscribed in about a second and have no memory of
doing it. So when you publish an 18-minute video you are not launching to 195
people — you are launching to roughly ten. That is far below the volume
YouTube needs to decide whether to widen distribution, which is why the seed
test in CAUSE 1 never gets past the seed.

The encouraging half: the subscribers who DO watch, watch hard — 130s AVD and
109.5% average view percentage, meaning rewatch. The audience is not bad. It
is small and unreachable, which is a different and more fixable problem.
