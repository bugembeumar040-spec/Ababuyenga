## Accuracy Audit (Fiqh Posture)

**Posture:** Mechanism, never verdict. No ruling issued in Kareem's voice.

### Checked Claims

1. **"2.5% of your savings"** ✅
   - Correct for cash, gold, silver, monetary wealth held 1 lunar year
   - NOT universal (agriculture 5–10%, livestock on schedules, rikaz 20%)
   - Script stays scoped to money/stored wealth ✅

2. **"Gold sitting in a drawer counts"** ✅
   - Deliberately worded (drawer = storage, not personal jewelry)
   - Shields against Hanafi/Maliki difference on jewellery
   - Description must clarify this distinction

3. **"Nisab ≈ 85g gold"** ✅
   - Correct but incomplete (silver nisab ~595g, much lower in modern money)
   - Description MUST give both options, flag silver as lower
   - Majority of scholars recommend silver nisab (benefits poor)

4. **"That's not a tax"** ✅
   - Scene 16B explicitly refuses the label: "That's not a tax. It isn't charity either."
   - Critical — if cut without context, changes meaning
   - Scenes 16A + 16B are inseparable (protected)

5. **"Money owed to you counts"** ✅
   - Immediately-due debts, not 25-year mortgage balance
   - VO says "owe now" — loads this precision
   - Keep word "now" ✅

6. **"Subtract what you owe now"** ✅
   - Same precision: immediately-due liabilities only
   - Do NOT trim to "subtract your debts" (would become wrong)

7. **"Wealth in use is exempt"** ✅
   - Accurate for personal-use assets
   - Does NOT exempt investment property or assets held for resale
   - Description should clarify: "primary residence is exempt; rental flat is not"

### Fiqh Risk Mitigation

- ✅ No verdict in VO (scenes 05/06 are mechanism, not ruling)
- ✅ Every differed detail pushed to description (jewellery, nisab, investment property)
- ✅ All claims scoped to agreed ground
