states mechanisms as plain fact and lets the viewer supply the outrage. Full

[05] Interest rewards you for not using money.

[06] Zakat charges you for not using it.

[08] So money that sits still shrinks.

[09] Put it to work — a trade, something real —

[10] It never asks you to give wealth away.

[12] Money owed to you counts. Investments count.

ACCURACY — the claims that need to survive a comment section
================================================================================

This topic has a far higher comment-risk profile than Klarna or credit cards,
because zakat is an act of worship with four schools of detailed fiqh behind it,
not a product you can describe mechanically. The script's posture — mechanism,
never verdict — is doing heavy lifting. Hold it.

1. "CHARGES YOU" / "THE ONLY TAX ON MONEY THAT SITS STILL"
   The biggest risk in the film. Zakat is the third pillar and a right of the
   poor (haqq al-faqir), not a tax and not a penalty. The script never calls it
   a tax in your own voice except to REFUSE the label — [16B] opens "That's not
   a tax. It isn't charity either." That rebuttal must survive the edit. If you
   cut it for time, you have to cut [16A] with it, or you ship a video that
   calls zakat a tax and never takes it back. Do not separate those two scenes.
   Put the pillar framing explicitly in the description.

2. "TWO AND A HALF PERCENT"
   Correct for cash, gold, silver, trade goods and monetary wealth held one
   lunar year. NOT universal across all zakatable wealth — agricultural produce
   is 5% or 10% depending on irrigation, livestock runs on its own schedules,
   and buried treasure (rikaz) is 20%. The VO is scoped to money and stored
   wealth throughout and never claims 2.5% is the only rate. Keep that scoping.

3. "GOLD SITTING IN A DRAWER COUNTS"
   Deliberately worded. Gold held as stored wealth is agreed. Gold JEWELLERY in
   regular personal use is genuinely differed — the Hanafi position is that it
   is zakatable, while the Maliki, Shafi'i and Hanbali positions generally
   exempt jewellery kept for permitted personal use. "Sitting in a drawer" keeps
   you on agreed ground and is consistent with the film's stillness thesis. Do
   NOT let this drift in the edit to "gold counts" or "your jewellery counts" —
   that single word change starts an argument you cannot win in a reply. State
   the difference plainly in the description.

4. "NISAB ≈ 85g GOLD"
   Correct and standard. But there are two nisabs: roughly 85g of gold, or
   roughly 595g of silver, and the silver nisab is FAR lower in modern money —
   it brings many more people into obligation. A large number of scholars and
   charities recommend the silver nisab precisely because it benefits the poor.
   The VO gives the gold figure for pace; the description must give both and say
   the silver threshold is lower. If you only fix one thing from this section in
   the description, fix this one — it is the most likely single correction you
   will receive.

5. "SUBTRACT WHAT YOU OWE NOW"
   The word "now" is load-bearing. You generally deduct immediately-due debts,
   not the entire outstanding balance of a long-term liability like a 25-year
   mortgage. If the VO ever becomes "subtract your debts", the claim becomes
   wrong. Do not trim that word.

6. "WEALTH IN USE IS EXEMPT"
   Accurate for personal-use assets: primary residence, personal vehicle, tools
   of trade, household goods, clothing. Note the boundary the film doesn't have
   time for — property bought as an INVESTMENT or held for resale is treated
   differently from the home you live in. Worth one line in the description
   because "so my rental flat is exempt?" is a predictable comment.

7. WHERE IT GOES
   The eight categories of recipient are specified in Surah At-Tawbah (9:60).
   Not in the VO — no room — but naming the ayah in the description is cheap
   credibility and pre-empts "who decides who gets it?"

8. THE CENTRAL RHETORICAL CLAIM — "interest rewards stillness, zakat charges it"
   This is an economic characterisation, not a fiqh ruling, and it is defensible:
   interest accrues to capital held, zakat is levied on wealth held through a
   hawl. It is a framing, and the script presents it as a framing. It does not
   claim zakat's PURPOSE is monetary velocity — that would be a theological
   claim about divine intent and you should not make it. "Circulation rule" in
   [16B] is the strongest formulation you can safely use. Do not upgrade it to
   "that's why it exists."

9. NO VERDICTS, AS EVER
   The script issues no ruling in your own voice at any point, exactly as the
   Klarna and credit card packs held the line. Zakat's obligation is not
   differed so stating it is safe; every DETAIL below that is differed, and the
   script stays on the agreed ground in all of them. Keep it that way, and route
   every "what about X?" comment to "differed — ask your local scholar." That
   reply costs you nothing and wins you the thread.
