## 2. Critical issues found

**1. The same four bullets appeared three times.** Your three most recent roles — Emaar Malls
Group, GTS BLC, and KBC Technologies — carried virtually identical bullet lists, changed only
by verb tense. This consumed most of page 1 and read as copy-paste. Worse, it *hid* your best
story: you held the same Dubai Mall post through two service-provider transitions and were then
hired directly by Emaar. That is a retention signal a hiring manager will value, and it was
invisible. Now it is a single block with the title progression stacked beneath it, plus an
explicit bullet naming the retention.

**2. Four pages for an operations role.** First-pass screening is measured in seconds. Page 4
contained exactly two lines: "Expected Graduation in September 2026" and "Social Media:
@Ahmed Abdallah".

**3. The headline numbers have no home.** The profile claims *15% operational cost reduction*
and *25% customer retention growth*, but no bullet anywhere supports either. The only evidenced
figure in the whole document was **10%** (Al Alee, daily operating expenses). An interviewer
will ask where the 15% came from. See §5 — this is the highest-value fix left.

**4. No scale anywhere.** Not one team size, budget value, headcount, area, or footfall figure.
In FM, scale *is* the qualification. "Supervised a team of soft services staff" and "led a team
of accommodation specialists" tell a recruiter nothing — 4 people or 140?

**5. Education was a dumping ground.** A degree, a high-school diploma, a carpet-cleaning
course, BICSc, CHHM, and a **driving licence** were all filed under "Education". A driving
licence listed as education reads as carelessness. Split into *Education* and
*Certifications & Training*.

**6. Degree level never stated.** The Kampala International University entry read
"Business Administration and Management | General | Business Administration" — it never says
Bachelor, Diploma, or Certificate. Many ATS filters screen literally on "Bachelor". Fix this.

**7. Self-rated skill levels were hurting you.** The template had you declaring *Intermediate*
at Problem-Solving, Collaboration, Accountability, and Risk Management. No recruiter rewards
that; several will screen on it. Removed the ratings, kept every skill name.

**8. Tense and grammar.** The current role mixed third-person present ("Coordinates",
"Collaborates", "Manages") with past ("Conduct regular facility inspections... developed and
implemented"). Resumes take first-person implied voice — present tense for the current role,
past for prior ones. Normalised throughout.

**9. Typos and casing.** Corrected: `FM- Assistant Manager` → Assistant Manager — Facilities
Management · `Housekeeping Incharge` → Housekeeping In-Charge · `Level 2 Bicsc` → BICSc Level 2 ·
`British Institute of cleaning science` → British Institute of Cleaning Science ·
`Certified hospitality Housekeeping Manager` → Certified Hospitality Housekeeping Manager ·
`Jinja Sec School` → Jinja Secondary School · `Holiday Homes Unit- Support Services Lead` →
Support Services Lead — Holiday Homes Unit.

**10. Dead contact data.** "Social Media: @Ahmed Abdallah" names no platform and resolves to
nothing. Your LinkedIn URL is already in the header, which covers it.

**11. Missing UAE-market standards.** No nationality, visa status, notice period, or languages.
These are expected on Gulf resumes and their absence causes friction with agencies. See §5.

**12. No target title.** Nothing under your name told the reader what job you want. Added:
*Facilities & Soft Services Operations Specialist | Luxury Retail, Residential & Hospitality*.

## 4. What changed, and what was removed

**Removed, as instructed:**

  "Expected Graduation in September 2026" line, which belonged to that entry.

**Removed by judgement — tell me if you want any of these back:**

- "Social Media: @Ahmed Abdallah" — unresolvable handle; LinkedIn already in header.

**Consolidated, not deleted.** The original had 45 bullets, of which ~12 were literal duplicates
across the three Emaar-site roles. The one-pager carries 18 bullets; related points were merged
rather than dropped. I audited this line by line — **every employer, title, date, location, and
distinct claim from the original survives.** (One casualty caught during that audit and
restored: Ejadah's cross-functional collaboration point.)

**Note on length.** A truly one-page version of 8 roles and 13+ years cannot also use a 10pt
body font. The one-pager sets at 8.6pt — legible in print and on screen, but dense. The
full-length version is set larger and more comfortably. Use the one-pager for applications and
job portals; the full version suits agency submissions and internal HR files where two pages
are normal.

## 5. Open items — these need your input

These are the highest-value remaining improvements, and I could not write them without
inventing facts.

1. **Where did the 15% cost reduction happen?** Which role, and against what baseline? It needs
   to sit in a bullet, not just the profile.
2. **What does "25% customer retention" refer to?** Client contracts renewed? Tenant retention?
   The phrase is ambiguous for an FM role and an interviewer will probe it.
3. **Team sizes.** How many staff did you supervise at Ejadah, Farnek, FitRepublik, and Al Alee?
   Even approximate ranges transform those bullets.
4. **Dubai Mall scale.** Area covered, number of cleaning staff coordinated, or daily footfall
   served — any one of these anchors your current role.
5. **Budget responsibility.** Any figure you controlled or influenced (AED/year), at any role.
6. **The KIU qualification** — Bachelor's degree, Diploma, or Certificate? Exact award name.
7. **BICSc dates** — "01/2022 – 01/2025" is an unusual span for a Level 2 award. Is that a
   validity period or a typo?
8. **UAE market standards** — nationality, visa status, notice period, and languages
   (with levels). Worth a compact line at the foot of the resume.

Send me answers to any of these and I'll fold them in and re-render.
