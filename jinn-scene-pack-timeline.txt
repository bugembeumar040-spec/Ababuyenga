WHAT THIS FILE IS

jinn-scene-pack.txt carries the style rules, the palette reasoning and the
thumbnail spec — read that one once. THIS file is the working sheet: the global
style block and the global negative are appended to every single prompt, so you
never have to keep a negative field loaded or remember which rules apply. Copy
one block, paste, generate, move on.

Each block also carries the exact voiceover it is cut to, lifted verbatim from
jinnscriptCOPYPASTE.txt v3. The line marked ► is the line the image lands on —
that's the frame the cut is built around. The lines under it are the rest of the
VO the shot has to hold through, so you know how long the image is on screen
doing work.

  ORDER. Generate S02 first, approve it, lock the seed — it sets paper tone, ink
  weight, wash temperature and light angle for all 29 others. Then S20. Then
  work in order.

  OPTIONAL CUTS. CUT 1 → drop S07 and S16. CUT 2 → drop S14 and S15.

  THREE SILENCES. S09, S23 and S28 sit under the three manually spliced silences
  in the VO. Those three images hold alone with no narration over them. They
  cannot be ordinary.
