to shoot this Short. Pick one — do not mix them, the idioms don't cut together.

                    generations, no temporal artefacts, frames reusable as
                    thumbnails and as b-roll in the long-form cut.

House style, character sheet, palette, caption spec and the burn-in-the-numbers
rule are carried over unchanged. AMIR and the shopkeeper are the same builds —
reuse your locked reference frames, do not regenerate them from scratch.

TIMINGS BELOW ARE TARGETS, NOT THE CUT. Record the VO first, then re-derive the
real IN/OUT points into video/src/halalcard/beats.ts before you generate
anything on Track A. Same rule as Deadbeat.

TRACK B IS THE RECOMMENDATION ON THIS ONE. See the clip-length note under the

================================================================================
READ THIS FIRST — THE VIDEO IS NOT THE ONE YOU PROMISED
================================================================================

Deadbeat closes on "Follow — the halal card fix is next." That caption promised
a product. The product does not exist.

There is no Sharia-compliant credit card available to a UK retail customer. Not
from Al Rayan, not from Gatehouse, not from Nomo. The structures that exist in
other markets — bai al-inah, tawarruq, ujrah — never crossed into UK retail
banking, in significant part because two of the three are themselves contested.

That is not a problem with the video. That IS the video.

The strongest thing this script has is a genuine reversal at the midpoint: the
viewer arrives wanting a card and leaves understanding the category was the
mistake. You cannot fake that beat and you do not have to — it is simply true.
The back half then does the thing no one else in this niche does, which is name
what you actually lose by leaving credit cards behind, and how to replace it.

So the promise is still paid. "The halal card that actually exists" is delivered
in scene 06. It is just a debit card, and the honesty of that is the film.


================================================================================
CONTINUITY — three arguments are now spent
================================================================================

  BURNED #1 — MERCHANT FEE PASS-THROUGH.  The Klarna spine. Gone.

  BURNED #2 — MINIMUM PAYMENTS AND COMPOUNDING.  The old 198-view upload. Gone.

  BURNED #3 — THE BUSINESS MODEL.  Deadbeat's entire spine: deadbeat vs
  revolver, the balance is the product, the limit that arrives unasked. You
  published this one video ago. This script must not re-explain why the bank
  wants you carrying a balance. It assumes the viewer knows, because the two
  videos before it are what taught them.

That constraint is what forces this into being a pure fix video, which is
exactly what the series needs in the third slot. Diagnosis is finished. Nothing
in scenes 01–11 diagnoses anything.

SERIES CLOSE. This is the last Short in the arc. It does not promise a Part 4.
It points at the long-form — "The Truth About Credit in the UK" — which is where
the fiqh nuance, the product comparison and the full Section 75 detail live at
proper length. That handoff is the whole reason the series exists.

CALLBACK. Scene 11 burns BALANCE £0.00, the same figure that closed Deadbeat.
Deliberate. Three videos ending on one number is the cheapest series signature
available to you, and the viewer who has seen both will feel it land.

ACCURACY — the four claims that need to survive a comment section
================================================================================

"IN BRITAIN, THERE ISN'T ONE" — the strongest claim in the film and the one to
re-check before publishing. No UK retail Islamic bank currently offers a
Sharia-compliant credit card; the contested structures used in Malaysia and the
Gulf never crossed into UK retail. This is a market-availability claim, not a
principle, and market availability changes. Check Al Rayan, Gatehouse, Nomo and
UBL directly the week you publish. If one has launched something, the video
still works — the line becomes "there is almost one" — but do not discover that
in the comments.

"MOST SCHOLARS CALLED TWO OF THEM A COSTUME" — bai al-inah and tawarruq are
disapproved by the majority within the Hanbali, Maliki and Hanafi schools, on
the reasoning that they are sale contracts structured to arrive at interest-
bearing debt by another route. Ujrah, the fee-based structure, has the widest
acceptance of the three and is the one most commonly defended — which is why the
VO says "two of those structures" and not "all three". That precision matters;
do not let it get trimmed in the edit. Note also that ujrah is not uncontested
either, and the description should say so.

The posture holds: the VO reports what scholars concluded. It does not adopt a
position in your voice. Same line as Klarna and Deadbeat. On a question this
differed, reporting is the only defensible register, and it is also the one that
cannot be quoted back at you.

"SECTION 75" — Consumer Credit Act 1974. The card issuer is jointly and
severally liable with the retailer where the item costs over £100 and no more
than £30,000. It applies to credit cards, not to debit or prepaid cards. One
detail worth putting in the description because almost nobody knows it: you only
need to have paid part of the price on the card — a deposit is enough to bring
the whole purchase within it. The debit-card equivalent, chargeback, is a Visa /
Mastercard / Amex scheme rather than a statutory right, has no minimum or
maximum, and typically runs to 120 days. "Not law" is the honest distinction and
the caption says exactly that.

"REPORT YOUR RENT" — Canopy and CreditLadder both report rent payments to credit
reference agencies by reading tagged payments from your bank. Their free tiers
differ: Canopy reports to two agencies free, CreditLadder reports to one free
with the full set paid. Do not name a specific free tier on screen, it will
change. And carry the caveat into the description, because leaving it out would
be the dishonest version: rent reporting records missed payments too. It is a
credit file, not a credit boost.
