================================================================================
FINANCE % DECODED — CAR FINANCE PROMPT PACK — v3 DELTA
What survives the rewritten VO, what breaks, what has to be built
Reads on top of carfinancepromptpack.txt v2 · does not supersede it
================================================================================

SHORT ANSWER

Mostly, yes. Six of the eleven scenes hold unchanged, three are re-taskable
using art you have already specified, one is cut, and one needs rebuilding. The
style bible, cast, palette, caption system, negative-prompt strategy and the
faces-vs-institution rule all survive completely intact — none of that was ever
script-dependent and none of it should be touched.

Two things do break, and one of them matters:

  · A02 IS CUT, AND A02 WAS THE THUMBNAIL. The rewritten VO removes the
    logbook/keeper argument entirely, which was that scene's only job.
  · THE SCENE COUNT IS NOW WRONG BY ABOUT HALF. Eleven scenes over 58 seconds
    is a 5.3s average cut. Eleven scenes over the new 2:15 is a 12.3s average,
    which is a dead Short. You need ~20 beats.

The good news on that second point: most of the gap is filled by panel and
caption variants over stills you are already generating. Real new generations
needed: four, maybe five. Details in §5.

1. WHAT THE v2 PACK GOT RIGHT — keep all of this
================================================================================

Said plainly because the rest of this file is corrections and the ratio would
otherwise mislead. The v2 compliance audit is good work and its central catch
is real.

ITEM 11 IS CONFIRMED. I checked it against the repo. credit-card-prompt-pack.txt
line 129 carries "facial features, eyes, mouth, nose, lips, eyebrows, face,
portrait" in its negatives, and lines 118, 167 and 196 explicitly instruct
COMPLETELY BLANK heads on AMIR and the clerk, over a near-black macro ground.
The uploaded pack's account of that error is accurate on every point, and
inverting the negatives to defend the faces is the correct fix.

ALSO CORRECT AND WORTH KEEPING:
  · The faces-vs-institution rule. It is the strongest device in the visual
    language and it does moral work no caption can do.
  · Stills as the primary track, animation only where motion is the argument.
    The three chosen for Track B (A06, A07, A08) are the right three.
  · Frame-for-type discipline — top fifth and bottom fifth clear on every
    prompt. Keep this on every new scene below.
  · The CAP 1 / CAP 2 / PANEL / PILL system, and the one-PILL-per-film rule.
  · Per-scene negatives rather than one global block.
  · Keeping the FCA redress material out of the VO and captions so it can be
    edited post-publish. Re-check it before you ship — the pack's status note
    is dated 2 July and today is 11 August.

INDEPENDENT CORROBORATION ON TITLES. The v2 packaging section reached the same
conclusion my channel audit did, from the same evidence: interrogative-plus-
parenthetical beats bare declarative, Pension against the 24–39 block. Two
passes agreeing from different directions is about as good as this gets.

2. THREE ERRORS IN v2 THAT ARE INDEPENDENT OF MY REWRITE
================================================================================

2.1  CAP 1 ON SCENE 02 CONTRADICTS THE PACK'S OWN ACCURACY SECTION.
     Caption: "THE LOGBOOK WAS NEVER YOURS." Accuracy section, correctly: the
     customer IS the registered keeper and the V5C states it is not proof of
     ownership. Both cannot be true. The logbook is issued to you; what is
     never yours is title. The pack diagnosed this precisely and then shipped
     the wrong version into CAP 1 and into the VO at line 584. Moot now — the
     scene is cut — but it would have gone out.

2.2  "UNCHANGED FROM v1 — THE MECHANISM WAS NEVER THE PROBLEM."
     The VO header's stated assumption. The channel data says otherwise: the
     terse 17–58s aphoristic format is the block doing 24–50 views, and the
     credit card pack shipped into it at 39. Not a mechanism problem, a format
     problem. Full workings in pcp-balloon-vo.txt §1.

2.3  "LENGTH 58.0s (SLATE RULE: 30–60s)."
     That slate rule is the single most expensive line in the pack. Every video
     the channel has published inside it has underperformed; every video at
     2:10–3:00 has done 700–1,200. The rule is inverted and should be struck.

3. SCENE-BY-SCENE VERDICT AGAINST THE REWRITTEN VO
================================================================================

  A01  HAND IT BACK                                          HOLDS UNCHANGED
       Still the hook, still the first line, still the right image. The
       rewritten opener adds "you paid interest on the piece you were always
       going to give away" as a second sentence — that gets its own beat (see
       §5, B02), A01 does not change.

  A02  NEVER YOURS — the document tug-of-war                            CUT
       Its entire content is the keeper/owner argument, which the rewrite
       removes on accuracy grounds. There is no line left for it to sit under.
       Do not try to re-caption it: two figures holding one document says
       "disputed ownership" and nothing else, and ownership is no longer the
       claim. See §4 for the thumbnail consequence.

  A03  THAT PART'S FINE — Sami, relaxed                    HOLDS, BUT MOVES
       Survives intact as art. Moves from 0:10 to roughly 1:58 — the concession
       is now objection-handling rather than an early disarm. This is not a
       downgrade: Part 8 puts its objection beat at 2:03 of 3:00, about 70%
       through, and it is that channel's best-performing video. The v2 note
       that scene 03 is load-bearing is still true; it is just load-bearing
       later. Consider one extra Sami frame so the beat can breathe over two
       cuts instead of one long hold.

  A04  THE PIVOT — Kareem to camera                    HOLDS AS A DEVICE
       The line under it is gone ("here's the part that isn't"). The device is
       needed more than before, not less: the rewrite adds first-person
       narration ("that was my first question too"), which is a Kareem-to-lens
       moment and is the warmth the format runs on. Generate two more
       expressions off the same reference — QUESTIONING and WARM. v2's claim
       that Kareem should appear exactly twice no longer holds; three or four
       is right at this length.

  A05  THE BALLOON — the absurd block                 HOLDS, AND IMPROVES
       Gets materially better. The old VO had no numbers, so the block was pure
       metaphor. The rewrite quantifies it — £13,000 against a £27,000 financed
       amount — so the block can now carry a real proportion and a real panel.
       Draw it to scale: roughly half the row's total mass, not eight units.

  A06  YOU PAY ON IT — the band crossing everything          HOLDS ★
       Now the most important frame in the film. The rewrite gives it £103 a
       month, £3,700, and "nearly two-thirds of all the interest." Move the
       PILL here from A07. This is the beat the video exists for.

  A07  NEVER OWN IT — grey hand lifts the block out          HOLDS
       Lands under "charged on a piece of car you never intended to own, and
       never did." Unchanged. Becomes the thumbnail — see §4.

  A08  FLOW ONE — tokens travelling to the institution     RE-TASK
       Its VO is gone; the "two flows" construction is cut. But the image —
       uniform tokens flowing one way from a person to the grey figure — is
       exactly the Part 1 recap the rewrite opens with: "banks rent out money
       and call it interest." Same prompt, new position, new captions. No
       regeneration.

  A09  FLOW TWO — Adam vs Bilal split frame               REBUILD
       Broken as specified. The Adam/Bilal comparison is outright-owner versus
       financed, and the rewrite never makes that comparison. What it does make
       is PCP versus Ijara. Rebuild the same split-frame layout with SAMI on
       the right instead of BILAL, holding nothing, with the grey figure
       carrying the car on his side. New prompt in §5, D01. The layout,
       the centre rule, the matched scale and the panel position all carry over.

  A10  PAGE ONE                                       HOLDS UNCHANGED
       "Go and find your balloon. It's on page one of your agreement —
       optional final payment." The prompt already specifies one empty ruled
       box and a finger resting on it. Nothing to change.

  A11  THE CLOSE                                     HOLDS, PLUS ONE CARD
       Fine as is. Add a final type card after it for "No jargon. Just
       mechanisms." — the series signoff, which v2 predates.

  TALLY   6 hold · 2 re-task without regenerating · 1 rebuild · 1 cut
          Plus ~9 new beats, of which 4–5 need actual generations.

CASUALTY NOTE — BILAL. He loses his only scene. The rewrite's comparison is
PCP against Ijara, and Ijara's owner is the financier, so there is no slot for
an outright cash owner. He stays in the A11 group shot. If you want him back
in the body of a film, he is the natural lead for a "buy it outright" episode,
which the series does not yet have.

4. THE THUMBNAIL — the real cost of cutting A02
================================================================================

A02 was doing three jobs at once: one object, two contradictory labels, and
Adam's QUESTIONING face. That was a good thumbnail and it is gone.

REPLACE IT WITH A07. It is the only surviving frame that carries a face, a
number and the argument simultaneously: Adam WORRIED in profile, the grey hand
lifting the terracotta block out of frame, the gap left behind on the baseline.
Set the panel large —

    INTEREST PAID ON THIS PART
    £3,700

— and let the empty stretch of baseline do the rest. It matches the pattern v2
correctly identified in the Student Loan thumbnail, where the WORRIED face
carries the frame.

SECOND CHOICE is A06 with the band crossing the big block, panel reading £103 A
MONTH. Stronger diagram, weaker thumbnail — no face, and every winning
thumbnail on the channel has one.

Generate A07 first now, not A02. It becomes the ADAM reference frame that every
other Adam scene locks to, which was A02's other job.

8. STILL OUTSTANDING
================================================================================

Unchanged from v2's own list, and now confirmed against the repo:
credit-card-prompt-pack.txt carries the v1 errors in full — inverted negatives
at line 129, blank-head instructions at 118/167/196, near-black macro
throughout. It has already shipped once as "The Bank Doesn't Want You
Debt-Free" (39 views). The shelved halal-card pack is presumably the same.

Rebuilding those to v2's style bible is worth doing, but it is not the first
thing. On the channel's own numbers the format and length decisions are worth
more than the render idiom: the Deadbeat frame proves you already correct the
idiom by hand at production time, and it still did 39 views. Fix the length and
the title pattern first, then come back for the packs.
