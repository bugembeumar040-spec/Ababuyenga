STYLE CHANGED. Everything below replaces the previous house style. The old
packs (credit-card, halal-card, long-form) describe stylised low-poly 3D, matte
clay shading, near-photorealistic texture, deep near-black backgrounds, hard
raking key light, shallow depth of field and COMPLETELY BLANK featureless heads.
None of that is current. Do not generate from those prompt files — use
flow-image-prompts-v2.txt.

WHAT CARRIED OVER, unchanged:
  · Burn the numbers in the edit. Never generate text, digits or logos.
  · 9:16 for Shorts, 16:9 for long-form.
  · Subject in the lower two-thirds (9:16) / on a vertical third (16:9).
  · The editorial posture. Nothing about the scripts changes.


================================================================================
HOUSE STYLE v2
================================================================================

Flat 2D vector cartoon illustration. Clean uniform dark brown outlines of even
weight. Flat solid fills, no gradients, no texture, no grain. Simple cel shading
in a single darker tone of the base colour. Warm cream background. Soft pale
elliptical shadow under figures and objects. Friendly rounded shapes. Modern
explainer-illustration look.

================================================================================
THE CAST
================================================================================

KAREEM — THE HOST
  Black hair, short and slightly tousled with a raised front. Full dark beard
  with a little grey through it. Olive henley shirt, three buttons at the
  placket, sleeves rolled to the forearm. Grey trousers, dark brown shoes.
  Expressions on sheet: neutral, warm, serious, questioning.
  ROLE. Presenter. Carries the long-form cold open, the chapter transitions
  and the fiqh chapter. He is the only character who addresses camera.

ADAM — THE CARDHOLDER
  Brown hair, short and neat. Clean-shaven. Rust-red short-sleeve t-shirt,
  camel trousers, dark brown shoes.
  Expressions on sheet: neutral, pleased, worried, resolved.
  ROLE. The protagonist. Everything happens to him. He replaces the character
  formerly called AMIR in every earlier pack — same function, new build.
  His four expressions map onto the arc: pleased (the limit rises) → worried
  (the statement) → resolved (the close). Use them, they are the story.

SAMI
  Dark hair, short speckled stubble beard. Teal t-shirt, slate trousers.
  Medium tan skin.
  ROLE. The cash payer. He is the man in the long-form who pays cash and
  subsidises everyone else's instalment plan. Also the second customer wherever
  a scene needs one.

BILAL
  Dark brown hair, full thick dark beard. Khaki button-up shirt with sleeves
  rolled, espresso trousers. Tan skin.
  ROLE. The shopkeeper / market trader. A warm human merchant, deliberately
  contrasted against the grey Clerk. Where the old packs had one shopkeeper
  doing both jobs, the cast now splits them — and that split is the argument.

THE CLERK — THE INSTITUTION
  A plain mid-grey humanoid silhouette. No face, no features, no clothing
  detail. Same outline weight as the humans. Appears behind the barred counter.
  ROLE. The bank. Never named, never given a face, never warm.

  ►► THE CENTRAL DESIGN RULE. Humans have faces. The institution does not.
     Do not give the Clerk a face under any circumstances, and do not remove
     faces from the four named men. The whole visual argument sits on that
     contrast and it collapses if either side drifts.

DEVICES
  THE COUNTER — bank teller window, vertical dark bars, olive lower wall,
    terracotta counter surface, grey figure behind it.
  THE PAPER   — blank white card and an open blank statement with ruled lines
    and one empty box in a corner. Flat, top-down, soft drop shadow.

UNIVERSAL BLOCKS — paste into every generation
================================================================================

STYLE (append to every prompt):
Flat 2D vector cartoon illustration, clean uniform dark brown #3D2B24 outlines
of even weight, flat solid fill colours, no gradients, no texture, no grain,
simple cel shading in a single darker tone, warm cream #F7F0DF background, soft
pale elliptical shadow beneath, friendly rounded shapes, modern explainer
illustration style.

NEGATIVE — STANDARD (objects and the four named men):
photorealistic, photograph, 3D render, CGI, clay render, realistic texture,
fabric weave, wood grain, film grain, noise, gradients, glossy highlights,
specular reflections, volumetric lighting, dramatic shadows, dark background,
black background, cinematic lighting, depth of field, bokeh, blurry, sketchy
lines, uneven line weight, watercolour, oil painting, anime, manga, text,
lettering, numbers, logos, brand names, watermark, signature, extra fingers,
distorted hands, extra limbs, cluttered background, busy composition,
low resolution

NEGATIVE — INSTITUTION ADDENDUM (add ONLY on Clerk / counter shots):
facial features, eyes, mouth, nose, eyebrows, face, portrait, skin tone,
clothing detail, patterned clothing, friendly expression, smile

NEGATIVE — TEXT-RISK ADDENDUM (add on any printable surface):
embossed digits, card numbers, serial numbers, expiry dates, printed rows,
printed type, typography, signage, nameplates, house numbers, price text,
labels, barcodes
