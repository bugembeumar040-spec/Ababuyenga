SOURCE ANALYSIS — MAJOR THEME OF THE PARENT SPEECH
================================================================================

Both voice notes are an Arabic dars/khutbah. Transcribed with Whisper large-v3.
The theme is unambiguous:

    إفشاء السلام — SPREADING THE GREETING OF PEACE.

The speaker builds it in two halves, and a script has to carry both.

HALF ONE — THE CASE (file 1, 0:00–2:45). Why salaam is not small.
  · Allah honoured the human being, and the greatest honour named is that
    Allah Himself greets him.
  · The angel-keepers greet them at the gates of Jannah.
  · The people of Jannah greet one another with it — تحيتهم فيها سلام.
  · Therefore the Prophetic command: "لا تدخلوا الجنة حتى تؤمنوا... أفشوا
    السلام بينكم."

HALF TWO — THE PRACTICE (file 1, 2:45–4:30 + all of file 2). What you do.
  · Salaam on arriving at a gathering AND on leaving it — "إذا جاء أحدكم
    المجلس فليسلم... وإذا قام فليسلم."
  · آداب السلام — who greets whom: the young greets the old, the passer-by
    the seated, the few the many.
  · Say it loud enough to be heard; don't single one person out; greeting a
    group covers all of them.
  · "تطعم الطعام وتقرأ السلام على من عرفت ومن لم تعرف."
  · File 2 anchors the duty side: وإذا حييتم بتحية فحيوا بأحسن منها أو ردوها.

Rev 1 of this script was built only from Half One and therefore read as pure
theology with no instruction. Rev 2 keeps the divine framing as the hook and
spends its back half on the practice, which is where the speaker spends his.

THE SCRIPT
================================================================================

[00:00–00:08]  HOOK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[curious] Two words. [pause] You say them every day without thinking.

[whispers] As-salamu alaykum.

[building] But do you know Who says them to you first?


[00:08–00:20]  THE DIVINE FRAME
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[reverent] Allah describes the believers on the Day they meet Him:

[slowly] [reverent] تَحِيَّتُهُمْ يَوْمَ يَلْقَوْنَهُ سَلَامٌ

[awe] [softly] "Their greeting, the Day they meet Him — is Peace."
[pause] Al-Ahzab, forty-four.


[00:20–00:27]  THE TURN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[serious] This isn't a habit. [pause] It's the language of Paradise.

[emphatic] And the Prophet ﷺ told us what to do with it *here*.


[00:27–00:40]  THE COMMAND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[dramatic tone] "You will not enter Paradise until you believe. And you will
not believe until you love one another."

[curious] Then he said — [pause] shall I show you what will make you love
each other?

[whispers] أَفْشُوا السَّلَامَ بَيْنَكُمْ

[emphatic] "Spread the salaam between you." [pause] Sahih Muslim.


[00:40–00:52]  THE DUTY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[serious] And when it comes to you — [pause] it is not optional.

[slowly] [resolute] وَإِذَا حُيِّيتُم بِتَحِيَّةٍ فَحَيُّوا بِأَحْسَنَ مِنْهَا أَوْ رُدُّوهَا

[firm] "When you are greeted — answer with better than it. [pause] Or at the
very least, return it."
[pause] An-Nisa, eighty-six.


[00:52–01:00]  THE CLOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[warm] They asked him which Islam is best. He said: [pause] feed people —

[gently] and give salaam to the one you know...

[tender] [whispers] and the one you don't.


================================================================================
DELIVERY NOTES FOR ELEVENLABS
================================================================================

VOICE.     Male, warm mid-low register, unhurried. Stability ~45, similarity ~75.
           Style exaggeration LOW (~15) — v3 over-acts religious text when pushed,
           and the tags below are already doing the work.

TAG STACKING. Where two tags are bracketed together — [slowly] [reverent] — v3
           reads them as one combined instruction. Keep the pacing tag first.
           Tags are direction, never spoken aloud.

THE ARC.   The tags are sequenced deliberately and shouldn't be shuffled:
           curious → whispers → building  (pull them in)
           reverent → awe                 (lift to the divine)
           serious → dramatic → emphatic  (land the command)
           resolute → firm                (the obligation, no softness here)
           warm → gently → tender/whisper (release)
           The close whispers because the open whispered. That rhyme is the
           reason the last line lands — don't replace it with [emphatic].

ARABIC.    Do NOT generate the Arabic in the same pass as the English. v3
           mispronounces Qur'anic Arabic and will not observe tajweed. Record
           the three Arabic pulls as human VO on their own track. If they must
           be synthetic, run a separate Arabic-locale pass and hand-check every
           ayah before publishing.

PACING.    Target 60s flat. English body runs ~150 wpm. If the read comes in
           long, cut the two surah citations ("Al-Ahzab, forty-four" /
           "An-Nisa, eighty-six") and burn them as on-screen text instead —
           never trim the ayat themselves.

MUSIC.     Bed under the English only. Duck to silence for each Arabic pull —
           the drop to dry voice is what makes them land.
