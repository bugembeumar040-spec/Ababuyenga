SOURCE ANALYSIS — MAJOR THEME OF THE PARENT SPEECH
================================================================================

The attached voice notes are an Arabic khutbah/dars. Whisper large-v3 transcript
of the first 166 seconds is coherent and the thesis is unambiguous:

    إفشاء السلام — SPREADING THE GREETING OF PEACE.

The speaker builds one argument in four moves:

  1. HONOUR.    Allah honoured the human being. And the greatest honour named
                is that Allah Himself greets him — "وأي إكرام أعظم أن يحيي الله
                عز وجل الإنسان."
  2. THE ANGELS. The angels enter upon them saying "سلام عليكم" —
                "فتستقبلهم الملائكة... يقولون سلام عليكم."
  3. JANNAH.    The people of Paradise greet one another with it —
                "ويتلقى أهل الجنة بعضهم بعضاً بالسلام / وتحيتهم فيها سلام."
  4. THE COMMAND. Therefore it is not etiquette, it is a door. The Prophet ﷺ:
                "لا تدخلوا الجنة حتى تؤمنوا... أفشوا السلام بينكم."

So salaam is presented as the greeting of Allah, the greeting of the angels and
the native language of Jannah — and the Prophetic instruction is to spread it
here, to those you know and those you do not.

The script below carries exactly that spine. Nothing is added to the argument.

THE SCRIPT
================================================================================

[00:00–00:07]  HOOK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[curious] Two words. [pause] You say them every day without thinking about them.

[whispers] As-salamu alaykum.

[warm] But do you know Who says them to you first?


[00:07–00:19]  MOVE 1 — ALLAH'S GREETING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[reverent] Allah describes the believers on the Day they meet Him:

[slowly] تَحِيَّتُهُمْ يَوْمَ يَلْقَوْنَهُ سَلَامٌ

[softly] "Their greeting, the Day they meet Him, will be — Peace."
[pause] Surah Al-Ahzab, forty-four.


[00:19–00:31]  MOVE 2 — THE ANGELS AT THE GATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[awe] And when the gates open, the angels who keep them say:

[slowly] سَلَامٌ عَلَيْكُمْ طِبْتُمْ فَادْخُلُوهَا خَالِدِينَ

[softly] "Peace be upon you. You have done well — so enter it, to stay forever."
[pause] Surah Az-Zumar, seventy-three.


[00:31–00:38]  THE TURN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[serious] The greeting of Allah. The greeting of the angels.
[emphatic] Paradise doesn't just contain peace — [pause] it speaks it.


[00:38–00:53]  MOVE 3 — THE COMMAND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[serious] So the Prophet ﷺ told us what to do with it here:

[slowly] لَا تَدْخُلُونَ الْجَنَّةَ حَتَّى تُؤْمِنُوا، وَلَا تُؤْمِنُوا حَتَّى تَحَابُّوا

[emphatic] "You will not enter Paradise until you believe. And you will not
believe until you love one another."

[curious] Then he said — shall I show you something that will make you love
each other?

[whispers] أَفْشُوا السَّلَامَ بَيْنَكُمْ

[emphatic] "Spread the salaam between you." [pause] Sahih Muslim.


[00:53–01:00]  CLOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[hopeful] The road to Jannah starts with a sentence you already know.

[gently] So say it. [pause] To the one you know —
[warm] and to the one you don't.

DELIVERY NOTES FOR ELEVENLABS
================================================================================

VOICE.     Male, warm mid-low register, unhurried. Stability ~45, similarity ~75,
           style exaggeration LOW (~15) — v3 over-acts religious text if pushed.

ARABIC.    Do NOT run the Arabic lines through the same generation as the
           English. v3 mispronounces Qur'anic Arabic and will not observe
           tajweed. Record the three Arabic pulls as human VO and drop them in
           on their own track. If they must be synthetic, generate them in a
           separate Arabic-locale pass and hand-check every ayah before use.

TAGS.      Tags are direction, not dialogue — they are consumed, never spoken.
           [pause] is a beat of roughly 0.4s; do not stack it with commas.
           Keep [whispers] on the two lines marked and nowhere else — it loses
           its effect the third time.

PACING.    Target 60s flat. English body runs ~150 wpm. If the read comes in
           long, the trim is the two surah citations, not the ayat.

MUSIC.     Bed under the English only. Duck to silence for each Arabic pull —
           the drop to dry voice is what makes them land.
