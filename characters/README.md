# Character bible

Cast for the AMIR films, in one locked house style.

## The style — never restate it loosely, paste it

Every sheet and every shot uses the identical style block held in
`flow_prompts_character_sheets.txt`. Bold dark outlines, smooth cel
shading, semi-realistic proportions, warm skin tones, muted desaturated
clothing, plain warm cream ground. This is the polished version, not the
flat cartoon of the earlier sheets.

## The cast

| Character | Read | Locked wardrobe |
|---|---|---|
| **AMIR** | protagonist, late 20s, reserved | charcoal button-up, sleeves rolled · dark grey trousers · brown shoes |
| **ELDER BROTHER** | mid 30s, warm, heavier | dark olive crewneck · indigo jeans turned once · brown boots |
| **SHOPKEEPER** | late 50s, weathered, patient | oatmeal shirt, sleeves pushed up · dark canvas apron · brown trousers |
| **THE LENDER** | 40s, cold, precise | slate-blue suit, no tie · white shirt · black shoes |
| **THE MOTHER** | late 50s, quiet | soft grey headscarf · charcoal tunic · loose dark trousers |
| **YOUNGER SISTER** | early 20s, alert | dusty-rose headscarf · olive tunic · loose dark trousers |

Wardrobe is the identity. Never change a garment between films — the
audience tracks the colour, not the face.

## Two cautions

1. **The faceless rule.** `credit-card-prompt-pack.txt` bans facial
   features outright and asks for blank heads. These sheets have faces.
   Use them for a series that shows faces; do not feed them as reference
   into a faceless pack or they will fight it.
2. **Feed the sheet, don't re-describe.** Once a sheet is approved, wire
   the image in as a reference for every shot of that character. A text
   re-description drifts by the third film.
