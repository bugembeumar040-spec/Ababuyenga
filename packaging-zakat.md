## Description

```
Two and a half percent of your savings was never yours. Here's the rule almost everyone gets wrong — and the two-minute fix.

Most people calculate zakat on their bank balance alone. That's the mistake. Money owed to you counts. Investments count. Gold sitting in a drawer counts. Your home, your car and your tools don't. Wealth in use is exempt. Wealth asleep is charged.

HOW TO CALCULATE ZAKAT — THREE STEPS
1. Add everything that has sat still for one lunar year (hawl)
2. Subtract what you owe now — debts due now, not the entire balance of a 25-year mortgage
3. If what's left clears the nisab, 2.5% of it is due

NISAB — READ THIS BEFORE YOU CALCULATE
There are two thresholds: roughly 85g of gold, or roughly 595g of silver. The silver nisab is far lower in today's money, so it brings many more people into obligation — and many scholars and charities recommend it for exactly that reason. This video quotes the gold figure for pace. Check both before you decide you are below the threshold.

WHERE THE DIFFERENCES ARE
• Gold jewellery in regular personal use is genuinely differed. The Hanafi position is that it is zakatable; the Maliki, Shafi'i and Hanbali positions generally exempt jewellery kept for permitted personal use. "Sitting in a drawer" in this video means stored wealth, not what you wear.
• 2.5% is the rate for money, gold, silver and trade goods. It is not the universal rate — agricultural produce is 5% or 10% depending on irrigation, and livestock runs on its own schedules.
• Your home is exempt. Property bought as an investment or held for resale is treated differently.

WHAT ZAKAT ISN'T
Zakat is the third pillar and a right of the poor (haqq al-faqir). It is not a tax and it is not charity — the eight categories of recipient are named in Surah At-Tawbah (9:60). This video describes it as a circulation rule: a mechanism that makes stillness expensive instead of rewarding it. That is a description of how it works, not a claim about why it was ordained.

No rulings are issued in this video. Every differed detail above is flagged as differed. For your own situation, ask your local scholar.
```
